OnDevice: Local AI Studio — Support

Help, safety reports and contact details.

Contact

Email mesutcydev@gmail.com, or open an issue at https://github.com/Mesutcydev/ios-local-llm/issues (a GitHub account is needed for issues). Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the model you were using. Do not include private conversations or credentials.

Report unsafe or offensive AI output

In the app, press and hold an AI response and tap Report; it opens this page. Then email us what happened and the app version. You do not need to send private text. Reports are reviewed and used to improve the safeguards. Conversations are stored only on your device, so we cannot see or remove them for you.

Common questions

Does the app send my prompts to the cloud?
No. Chat runs on your device with the model you chose. The network is used only for things you start or turn on: downloading models, optional web search (a permission sheet shows the exact query first), optional iCloud sync, and the optional local API and Mac bridge on your own network. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?
Open Settings in the app and choose Wipe all on-device data. It does not delete iCloud copies or records held by a web search provider; the privacy policy explains how to remove those.

Which devices and iOS versions work?
iOS 18 or later. Which models run, and how fast, depends on your iPhone's memory and storage. Models that are too large for your device are declined with a message instead of closing the app.

A model will not load or download.
Check free storage and your connection, close other apps, and try a smaller model. The download screen shows each model's size before you start.

Can the assistant see my calendar or reminders?
Only after you approve each request in the chat and allow access in the iOS permission prompt. You can decline, and you can turn access off in iOS Settings at any time. Voice conversations cannot use these tools.

What is the local API?
An optional server you turn on in Settings. It requires an API key, uses HTTPS, is meant for networks you trust, and stops when the app goes to the background.
